=== Neumaier decision (spec REQ 'fixed-tree ... Neumaier decision recorded from measured need') ===
  DECISION: Neumaier compensation NOT enabled (SHUD_NVEC_DETRED_NEUMAIER stays 0; plain fold).
  BASIS: Neumaier compensates summation ACCURACY WITHIN A FIXED ORDER (it reduces the rounding
  error of a given accumulation sequence). The measured E2-vs-E difference is a STEP-SEQUENCE
  divergence caused by the order CHANGE (tree vs serial fold), not by in-block rounding error;
  Neumaier cannot make two different summation orders agree and would not shrink this delta.
  Meanwhile the tightened A5 already PASSES at nse=1.0/kge=0.9999 with the plain tree, so there
  is no accuracy deficit to compensate. Adding Neumaier would only slow the reduction (extra
  FLOPs per element) for zero acceptance benefit. The compensated path is implemented and
  compile-time-available (SHUD_NVEC_DETRED_NEUMAIER=1) should a future case ever demand it.
