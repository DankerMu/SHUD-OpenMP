must be pinned to -O0 to bitwise-match the -O0-compiled SUNDIALS library serial
reductions that Config C uses. Without the pin the -O2 optimizer reassociates
the scalar accumulation → ~1 ULP drift → G-E1 vs-Config-C FAIL.
