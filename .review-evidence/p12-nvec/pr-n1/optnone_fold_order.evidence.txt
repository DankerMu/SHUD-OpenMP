P12-nvec PR-N1 — CORRECTED root-cause + fix evidence (supersedes the earlier
"-O2 reassociation" narrative, which was mechanically wrong: IEEE FP-add
reassociation is illegal at plain -O2 without -ffast-math and neither clang nor
gcc performs it).

TRUE MECHANISM: SHUD's -ffp-contract=off (B0 IEEE-754 lockdown) makes a
SHUD-compiled generic-API serial reduction diverge from the vendored SUNDIALS
*_Serial reductions ONLY on Apple clang / ARM, via two codegen effects:
  (1) it STRIPS the scalar FMA the vendored lib uses by default (no -ffp-contract
      flag in the lib build) — two roundings vs one;
  (2) at -O2 the non-FMA reduction auto-vectorizes into a different fold order.
On x86_64 / gcc the gcc-built lib is scalar non-FMA and -O2 -ffp-contract=off
already produces the same scalar non-FMA fold → the plain override matches.
SHUD_NVEC_NOOPT (optnone on clang / optimize("O0","no-tree-vectorize") on gcc)
is REQUIRED on clang/ARM and correctness-INERT on gcc/x86.
